School Acquaintance—Colors of Unity 2026

Explore the activities, programs, and memorable moments of the ISUFST San Enrique Campus School Acquaintance through photos and videos. Colors of Unity 2026 is a two-day campus activity of ISUFST San Enrique Campus held on September 17–18, 2026. The event highlights unity, participation, cultural activities, team building, performances, and different activities involving students and employees.

DAY 1

Day 1

The first day features different activities and programs that bring students and members of the campus community together.

Pag-Abiabi and Opening Parade with Employees Team Building

Morning Activities

The Day 1 morning session begins with the Parade of Colors, National Anthem, ISUFST Hymn, opening prayer, welcome remarks, and messages from university and community representatives. The session also includes the official declaration of Colors of Unity 2026, Unity Dance, introduction of the board of judges, reading of mechanics and criteria, and performances from participating colleges.

Acquaintance Program

Acquaintance Program

Street Dancing & Field Demonstration

Laro ng Lahi and Team Building Activities

Afternoon Activities

The Day 1 afternoon session features Laro ng Lahi activities for both employees and students. Activities include Big Beach Volleyball, Click and Clutch, Obstacle Relay, Sack Race, Longest Line, and Lusk Lusin, providing opportunities for participation, teamwork, and interaction among the campus community.

Day 2

The second day continues the School Acquaintance activities with additional programs and campus moments.

Morning Activities

The Day 2 morning session begins with the Monthly Mass scheduled at the Civic and Cultural Center. The activity provides an opportunity for the campus community to gather for prayer and thanksgiving before the continuation of the day's Colors of Unity activities.

Acquaintance Program

Monthly Mass

Mr. and Ms.Colors of Unity and LGBTQIA+ Pride Ambassador

Afternoon Activities

The Day 2 afternoon session continues with the Colors of Unity activities, beginning with the Parade of Colors, National Anthem, ISUFST Hymn, Doxology, welcome remarks, and messages. The program features Mr. and Ms. Colors of Unity and LGBTQIA+ Pride Ambassador activities, including production numbers, RIO-inspired beachwear, lip-sync battle, cultural attire, special performances, interviews, and the announcement of winners.
